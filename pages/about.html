Our Story

From a Vision to India's #1 Ed-Tech Platform

CogniTest was founded in 2017 by three IIT alumni who witnessed firsthand how geographical and economic barriers prevented talented aspirants from accessing quality exam coaching.

Starting with just 50 students and a team of 5 faculty members, we've grown into a platform that has helped over 45,000 students crack their dream government jobs — from IBPS clerks to RBI Grade B officers.

Our Mission
To democratize access to quality exam preparation through technology, making every student exam-ready regardless of location or budget.
Our Vision
A future where every qualified aspirant in India gets their deserved government position through merit-based preparation.

Journey & Growth

7 Years of Impact & Innovation

From a small startup to India's most trusted exam prep platform — here's how we grew.

2017 — Founded
CogniTest Launches with 50 Students
Founded by 3 IIT Bombay alumni in a 400 sq ft Pune office with a vision to democratize exam coaching.
2018 — Breakthrough
10,000 Students & First AI Mock Test Engine
Launched our proprietary AI-based adaptive testing engine. Crossed 10,000 enrolled students milestone.
2020 — Pivoting to Digital-First
Live Classes Platform & Mobile App Launch
During the pandemic, we became fully digital — launching live classes and our iOS/Android app. Students surged to 50,000.
2022 — National Recognition
Ranked #1 Ed-Tech for Government Exams
Featured in Forbes India's 30 Under 30 education list. Received NASSCOM EdTech Innovation Award.
2024 — Present
1,00,000+ Students & 45,000+ Selections
Today CogniTest serves 1 lakh+ active students with 200+ faculty, 15 exam categories, and a 95% student satisfaction rate.
