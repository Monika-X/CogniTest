Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2025. This policy explains how CogniTest collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Information 3. Information Sharing 4. Data Security 5. Cookies Policy 6. Your Rights 7. Data Retention 8. Children's Privacy 9. Policy Changes 10. Contact Us

CogniTest Pvt. Ltd. ("CogniTest", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information that applies to our educational platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you register, purchase, or use our services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, mobile number, profile photo, target exam, city
  • Payment Information: Billing address (payment card details are processed by PCI-DSS certified third-party processors — we do not store card numbers)
  • Academic Information: Educational background, target exam, previous exam attempts, performance data
  • Communications: Messages, doubts, and feedback submitted through our platform

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, test results, time spent, answer patterns
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Learning Analytics: Question-level performance data, accuracy rates, time per question, completion rates

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our educational services
  • Personalize your learning experience and generate AI-powered recommendations
  • Process payments and subscriptions
  • Send important service notifications, class reminders, and exam updates
  • Analyze platform performance and student learning outcomes
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising purposes without your explicit consent.

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors (payment processors, cloud services, analytics) bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you will be notified)
  • Aggregated Analytics: Non-personally identifiable aggregate data for research and educational purposes

We never sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

CogniTest implements industry-standard security measures including:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • ISO 27001 certified information security management systems
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
  • GDPR-compliant data processing practices

5. Cookies Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication and basic platform functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings (theme, language, RTL/LTR)

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or our Cookie Preference Center accessible from the footer.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, email privacy@cognitest.in or use the settings in your account dashboard.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Active account data: Retained for the duration of your account plus 3 years
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations
  • Test performance data: Retained for account lifetime (exportable on request)
  • Communication logs: Retained for 2 years from last interaction

8. Children's Privacy

CogniTest is designed for students aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it immediately. Parents who believe their child may have provided information should contact us at privacy@cognitest.in.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or prominent notice on our platform at least 30 days before changes take effect. Continued use of the platform after notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
